Engineering Integrity & Transparent Methodology

This tool is an educational signal processing prototype developed to demonstrate digital signal processing (DSP) and software architecture principles for electrical power systems. Event thresholds (Sag: <0.9 pu, Swell: >1.1 pu, Interruption: <0.1 pu, THD: ≥5.0%, VUF: ≥2.0%) are heuristic guidelines inspired by IEEE 1159 and IEC literature. It does not claim hardware-grade certification under IEC 61000-4-30 Class A, UTC/GPS clock synchronization, or real instrument transformer calibration.
